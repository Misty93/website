Behind She Hunts Threats

bacc.ing.techn.inf.

Blue Team Lead and Cybersecurity Consultant

She Hunts Threats is created and maintained by an experienced cybersecurity professional working in Blue Team operations, incident response, threat hunting, security architecture, detection engineering, and SOC development.

Behind the content is a real person with years of practical experience investigating incidents, analyzing suspicious activity, building detections, mentoring analysts, and helping organizations understand what is happening inside their environments and how to respond effectively.

What began as curiosity gradually became a hobby, then an obsession, and eventually a professional career in cybersecurity. Today, that journey includes leading a Blue Team while continuing to research, experiment, automate, write, and share practical knowledge with others.

My experience includes Microsoft Defender, Trend Vision One, Cybereason, QRadar, Wazuh, Stellar Cyber, Cisco ESA, Check Point Email Security, FortiMail, Trellix Email Security, FortiDeceptor, T-Pot, and various SIEM, EDR, SOAR, NDR, and email security platforms.

The technology changes, but the challenge remains the same: understand what happened, determine why it happened, and stop it from happening again.

I combine methodical analysis with creativity and curiosity, especially when it comes to strange logs, unusual attack patterns, automation, and the occasional gardening metaphor. 🌿

Before cybersecurity, I worked behind a camera. 📸🎬 Today, I spend my time hunting through logs, investigating incidents, building detections, and explaining why clicking that attachment was, in fact, a bad idea. 🧩

When I am not working, you will probably find me running, cycling, watching films, taking photos, building something in my home lab, breaking something in my home lab, or pretending that maintaining firewalls, SIEM platforms, honeypots, and servers is a perfectly normal hobby. 🧚🏻

I genuinely enjoy regex, which is usually the point where people stop asking questions. Being a Slytherin, I also felt contractually obligated to learn Python. 🐍

Creator of shehuntsthreats.com, cybercroatia.org, and cyber-europe.org. ⚡

Collector of certifications, suspicious IP addresses, and stories that begin with: “It was only supposed to be a small change.” 🌋

Because the best part of cybersecurity is not the technology itself. It is the moment when a pile of seemingly unrelated logs suddenly starts telling a story. 🖖🏻
